Harmonizing: turn VIIRS counts into “what MODIS would have counted”

VIIRS hotspots × factor = MODIS-equivalent hotspots The factor is learned where both satellites overlap (2012–2021), for every 2° region and for day and night passes. It works like converting °F to °C before comparing temperatures.

Then MODIS observed (2000–2011) and VIIRS converted (2012 →) join into one series. The sensor of origin no longer matters.

A fire baseline that survives the end of MODIS

  • Emergency managers and fire brigades: see when an area usually burns and whether this month is unusual. August 2026 ran at 20% of a normal August nationwide, a drop NOAA-20 confirms
  • Scientists: 25 consistent years to study trends and anomalies
  • Land managers and CONAE: an open, documented method that runs on public data
